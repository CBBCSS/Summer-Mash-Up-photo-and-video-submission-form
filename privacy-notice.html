Privacy Notice

Summer Mash-Up! Photo and Video Submissions

Please read this notice with your parent or guardian before submitting. The responsible project team should check this wording against the final arrangements before the form accepts real submissions.

Why are we collecting your information?

BBC Children’s Productions Ltd (“CPL”) will use the personal information you and your parent or guardian provide, including photos and videos, for the purposes of developing and producing Project Summer Mash-Up!, including reviewing submissions and considering whether contributions may be used in the project.

What information are we collecting?

CPL may collect and process:

  • Your first name or nickname and age group.
  • Your parent or guardian’s email address and any related contact details they provide.
  • The photos or videos you upload, including personal information that may appear or be heard in those files.
  • The description or other information you choose to include with your submission.
  • Consent confirmations and information needed to administer and review the submission.

Please only upload files you have permission to share. Avoid including addresses, school details, phone numbers or other unnecessary private information.

Who is the Data Controller?

CPL is the data controller for the project activity described in this notice. This means CPL decides what personal information is used for and how it is processed.

What is the lawful basis for processing?

The intended lawful basis for processing your and your child’s personal information for the purpose described above is consent. A parent or guardian should review and agree to a submission where the contributor is under 18. Consent may be withdrawn, subject to applicable limits and any steps already taken using the contribution.

Do we share your personal information with third parties?

Submissions may be accessed by CPL staff and relevant BBC staff involved in the project. This form also uses FormSubmit as a third-party service to receive submissions and handle file uploads. Information and files are processed by FormSubmit as part of delivering the service and may be stored in its dashboard and sent by email to the configured recipient. FormSubmit’s own terms and privacy arrangements apply to its processing. The project team should verify the provider’s current terms, storage arrangements, access controls and deletion options before accepting real submissions.

Retention Period

The intended administrative retention date for personal information is 18 December 2027. If a contribution is selected to participate in the programme, the contribution may be retained in the project archive in perpetuity, in line with the applicable project arrangements. Copies held by service providers should be deleted in line with agreed retention arrangements and the provider’s available controls.

Your Rights and how to contact us

If you have questions about how CPL handles personal information, or wish to exercise applicable rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection or restriction, contact DataProtectionEnquiries@bbc.co.uk.

You can contact the BBC’s Data Protection Officer at dataprotection.officer@bbc.co.uk.

If you are dissatisfied with the response to a data-protection complaint, you may contact the UK supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), at https://ico.org.uk/concerns.

Before launch: The responsible project team should confirm that this notice accurately reflects the final controller, service-provider, access, retention and deletion arrangements.
